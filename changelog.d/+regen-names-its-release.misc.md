`pytest --regen-schema` rebuilds the bundled artifacts from the dump of the shipped release only, found by its release train under `private_test_data/schemas/`, and stops with the reason when that dump is absent or ambiguous. It used to take whichever dump sorted newest, so the documented pre-commit step could have moved the shipped release as a side effect of which files a developer had synced. `JUNOSCFG_XSD_SOURCE` names a different dump on purpose.
