Added an opt-in test tier that runs junoscfg against Juniper's published Validated Designs corpus, about 1,500 configurations and five million lines across QFX, ACX, MX, PTX and SRX. Run it with `pytest --jvd`; it clones `github.com/Juniper/jvd` on first use, or reuses a checkout named by `JUNOSCFG_JVD_PATH`. The corpus found three defects that every committed fixture had missed, and the tier carries one regression guard for each.
