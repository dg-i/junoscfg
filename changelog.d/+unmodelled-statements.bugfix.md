A statement the schema does not model is kept whole and reported, where the set reader used to keep one token of it as a nokeyword leaf's value and drop the rest without a word. Field validation now names such a statement and its list entry on stderr and fails under `--strict`; `--no-field-validate` keeps the old silence. Anonymization refuses such a configuration, since it cannot rewrite what the schema does not name. On Juniper's published Validated Designs that is 69,122 of 2.2 million set lines, most of them `bridge-domains`, which the shipped schema does not yet model as a wrapper.
